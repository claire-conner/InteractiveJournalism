Hi! I'm Claire.

Journalist · Student · Sports & Policy Enthusiast

I'm a junior at Northwestern University's Medill School of Journalism, triple-majoring in Journalism, Political Science, and History. I love using data and technological tools to tell more compelling stories and improve my research.

My recent projects explored topics like private equity in college athletics and the rise of sports betting, but I spend most of my time in the broadcast booth and podcast studio. I'm a play-by-play announcer covering football, basketball, and women's lacrosse for WNUR Sports at Northwestern.

I am also passionate about policy research and advocacy work. Last summer, I interened at The Alliance for Children's Rights, a legal nonprofit that serves foster youth in Los Angeles. I built an automated policy tracker for the Alliance to help them stay updated on legislative changes that impact clients. I am excited to continue my policy work this summer as an intern for Senator Mark Warner's Washington, D.C. office.
